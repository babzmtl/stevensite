REANIMAL: video

For a school project, I decided to do the sound redesign for the game Reanimal. I did all the audio editing and the mix in Pro Tools. I recorded several sounds and used them to create my own custom sound effects and I also used some sound libraries. On top of that, I composed the soundtrack using Ableton.
